About Me

Hi, I'm Zach.

I'm a REALTOR® with Keller Williams Elevate, helping buyers, sellers and investors across the entire Bay Area — from San Francisco and the Peninsula to Silicon Valley and the East Bay.

Real estate here moves fast and the stakes are high. My job is to slow it down for you: keep every step clear, back every decision with real numbers, and fight for your best deal at the negotiating table. You'll always have a direct line to me — not a call center.

When I'm not with clients, I'm out touring homes and neighborhoods for my YouTube channel, Living in Silicon Valley, so you get local knowledge that's lived, not looked up.
