Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information zachbelman.com (the "site") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The site is operated by Zach Belman, REALTOR®, Keller Williams Elevate, CA DRE #02165828.

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number (optional), what you're interested in, and your message. Form submissions are delivered to Zach by our form provider, Formspree.
  • Booking a call: if you schedule a call, Calendly collects your name, email and any answers you provide, under Calendly's own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, the site uses cookies and similar technologies from:

  • Google Analytics, to understand how many people visit, which pages they view, how they found the site, and which visits lead to an inquiry.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram), to measure ads and to show ads to people who have visited the site.

These tools may collect your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and actions such as submitting the contact form, booking a call, or tapping the phone number or email address. They do not receive the contents of your message.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and provide real estate services.
  • To understand and improve the site.
  • To advertise on Google, Facebook and Instagram, including showing ads to people who have visited the site.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Allowing Google and Meta to use site activity for advertising may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can opt out as described below.

Who we share information with

Only service providers that help run the site and the business: Formspree (contact form), Calendly (scheduling), Google (analytics and advertising), Meta (advertising), and GitHub (website hosting). We may also disclose information if required by law. Your contact details are not shared with anyone else without your permission.

Your choices

  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this "do not sell or share" signal, the site does not load the Meta Pixel and turns off Google ad personalization for your visit.
  • Google: manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Meta: manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings under "Ad preferences".
  • Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • "Do Not Track": browsers' older "Do Not Track" setting has no agreed standard, so the site does not respond to it; it does honor Global Privacy Control, described above.

California residents

You may ask what personal information we have about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of sharing for advertising. Email zachbelman@kw.com with your request. We won't treat you differently for making one.

Keeping information

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to respond to you and to meet legal and brokerage recordkeeping requirements.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
